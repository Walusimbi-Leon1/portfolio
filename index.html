About Me

Who I am and what drives me

Storyteller. Builder. Dreamer.

I'm a Ugandan creator who believes in the power of words and code to shape the world. As an author, I write love novels that capture the beauty of African romance, and spiritual teachings that explore life's deepest questions.

As a developer, I build websites, apps, and interactive experiences — from music platforms to social games — using technology to connect people and bring ideas to life.

I'm also the founder of SGSS (Spiritual Growth & Scientific Studies), an organization dedicated to exploring the intersection of faith, science, and human potential.
